Exercise photos from Free Exercise DB (https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db),
released into the public domain under the Unlicense. Resized to 640 px wide.

Two exercises use the closest photos the library has: hanging knee raise shows the hanging leg raise (bent knees),
and single-leg dumbbell calf raise shows the standing dumbbell calf raise.

Closest photos used for the October 2026 additions:
- cable hip abduction shows a standing side leg raise (same movement without the cable),
- Copenhagen plank and side plank both show the side bridge,
- assisted pull-up machine shows a regular pull-up,
- dumbbell thruster shows the kettlebell thruster,
- battle rope slams use the battle rope waves photos.
Tall photos are centred on a blurred copy of themselves so the 3:2 frame doesn't crop the person.
